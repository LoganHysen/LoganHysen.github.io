Landscape ecologist · Conservation scientist

I am a PhD candidate in the Center for Systems Integration and Sustainability at Michigan State University studying how climate change and human activity shape wildlife habitat. I'm particularly interested in how humans and wildlife can coexist in shared landscapes, and how conservation decisions can support both. I study these questions across taxa, ecosystems, and geographies using a variety of spatial and quantitative approaches.

Some questions that guide my work

01

Habitat and connectivity

How do species select habitat and how does landscape change affect the connections between populations?

02

Human–wildlife coexistence

How does human activity shape wildlife habitat, and how can conservation account for the people who share the same landscapes as wildlife?

03

Coupled systems

How do processes, ecological or social, interact across local and global scales? How do systems influence each other across these scales?

04

Spatial conservation

How can we design and implement conservation strategies that effectively manage and protect biodiversity across spatial scales?
